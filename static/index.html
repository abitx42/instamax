🧠 Why CRF 18? Constant Rate Factor 18 is visually lossless. Instagram evaluates your upload with VMAF (perceptual quality). A high-VMAF source triggers Instagram's top delivery ladder tier with maximum bitrate.
🔬 Lanczos Super-Sampling: Downscaling 4K to 1080p via 3-lobed Lanczos packs 4 real sensor pixels into 1 crisp destination pixel, generating cleaner micro-edges than native 1080p recording.
👑 Flagship Profiles (iPhone 17 Pro Max / Galaxy S26 Ultra): Ingest servers prioritize top hardware signatures. Injecting an authentic profile routes the file through the cleanest encoding pipeline.
🎵 Audio at 48 kHz / -14 LUFS: 48 kHz is Instagram's native audio broadcast standard. -14 LUFS matches Instagram's loudness target so your sound never gets dynamically crushed.
📡 Zero-Loss Transfer: Transfer the output to your phone using LocalSend or Snapdrop. Never use WhatsApp or Telegram as they strip metadata and re-encode.
📱 Android Upload Setting: In Instagram: Profile → ☰ → Settings → Media Quality → "Upload at Highest Quality" = ON, Data Saver = OFF.
