🧠 Why CRF 18? Constant Rate Factor 18 is considered "visually lossless" — it gives complex scenes more bits automatically. Instagram evaluates your upload using VMAF (perceptual quality scoring). A higher CRF source → better VMAF score → cleaner served video.
🔬 Lanczos scaling is the highest-quality downscale algorithm. It preserves sharp edges better than Bilinear or Bicubic when going from 4K to 1080p — same method professional broadcast tools use.
🎵 Audio at 48 kHz / -14 LUFS — 48 kHz is Instagram's native broadcast sample rate (no resampling needed). The loudness filter targets -14 LUFS, matching Instagram's normalisation target, so your audio isn't turned down or dynamically crushed post-upload.
📐 Safe Zones matter. Instagram overlays your handle, caption, audio track, and like/comment/share buttons on top of the video. Keep all text, logos, and hooks inside the green safe zone (shown above) or nobody sees them.
📡 Transfer without compression — Use LocalSend or Snapdrop. Never WhatsApp/Telegram — they re-compress and wipe metadata.
📱 Android Instagram — Before uploading: Profile → ☰ → Settings → Media Quality → "Upload at Highest Quality" = ON, Data Saver = OFF.
👑 Flagship Profiles (iPhone 17 Pro Max / Galaxy S26 Ultra) — Instagram gives priority ingestion bandwidth to top-tier hardware signatures. Injecting an iPhone 17 Pro Max or Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra profile ensures Instagram routes the video into its best transcode profile.
